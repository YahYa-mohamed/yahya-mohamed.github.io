About me

I hold a bachelor's degree in Electronics Technology from the Faculty of Technology and Education at Helwan University, and I am currently studying Mechatronics Engineering at Tanta University. My professional experience is in railway operations, with a strong interest in railway systems, operational safety, industrial automation, and the practical use of data and AI in transportation.

I also create educational content about engineering drawing. I am building my technical portfolio across programming, data analysis, databases, and engineering projects, and I aim to connect these skills with real-world railway and automation challenges.
